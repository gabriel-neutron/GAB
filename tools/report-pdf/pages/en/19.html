3 Finance

Selling oil is not enough: Russia must also be paid, and then pay its foreign suppliers in turn. Before the war, most of these payments went through the dollar and the euro, and so through banks that the coalition controls. The question is where the money flows when those banks shut Russia out.

3.1 The rouble and the yuan

From 2022, the large Russian banks, including VTB and Sberbank, were designated by the United States53 and by the United Kingdom,54 and several were removed from SWIFT, on top of the freezing of their assets.55 Russian firms then moved from the currencies of "unfriendly" countries, such as the dollar and the euro, to the rouble and to other currencies, such as the yuan (figure 10).56
