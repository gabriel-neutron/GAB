With China, the shift goes further. At the end of 2023, the yuan settled as many exports as the rouble,58 and the rouble and the yuan settle almost all payments between the two countries.59 This choice protects the trade from sanctions, but it ties Russia to a partner that accounts for about 40% of its trade, while Russia accounts for only a few per cent of Chinese trade.60 On the report's hypothesis, China can therefore set its terms.

3.2 Payment relays

A payment in yuan is still exposed to US sanctions. A bank that settles in yuan still needs the dollar for the rest of its business. US Executive Order 14114, of December 2023, exposes to sanctions any foreign bank that helps the Russian military-industrial base. In June 2024, this risk extended to any significant transaction with VTB or Sberbank.61 Banks in third countries therefore withdrew. The Chinese bank that served as the main channel for Russian importers cut its ties with them in February 2024,62 Turkish and Emirati banks returned transfers or closed accounts,63 and in the summer of 2024 the Chinese state banks blocked Russian payments "en masse".64 Payments then moved through other relays, which the sanctions then followed:
