The nodes that sustain Russia’s war in 20263 · Finance

Some third countries let it happen. The United Arab Emirates welcomes Russian capital and companies without applying Western sanctions. The war against Iran, which has hit the Emirates since February 2026, does not change this line: in August, the Emirates cut their trade with Iran, not with Russia.77 Their banks limit some payments with Russia for fear of US sanctions, but no measure by the Emirati state against Russia is known.

The report therefore hypothesises that each wave of designations does not close a channel, but pushes payments towards a smaller relay, further from the dollar. The dollar does not disappear, because the sub-agents of A7 still pay in dollars, yuan, dirhams and euros.78 The lever therefore remains the bank that keeps this access, and that bank can leave the channel: in 2026, the EU removed three Tajik banks from its list, because they took measures.79
