6.2 Research leads

These limits point to directions for research:

–Measure the effect of sanctions: Compare monthly series of prices and export volumes with the dates of designation, to test whether a designation really widens the discount on Russian crude.

–Document payments and components: Link the companies of the database to payments and components, with amounts, using the alerts of the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN), US Treasury press releases and teardown studies of Russian weapons, to learn what volume passes through Kyrgyz banks and A7's sub-agents.

–Complete the mirror data: Look for customs data that cover the United Arab Emirates, Vietnam, Kazakhstan and Thailand, to learn what share of the priority goods for weapons passes through these countries.

–Date the changes in the fleet: Archive the history of AIS identities and compare it with the flag registers, to learn how long a designated vessel takes to change identity.
