How to read this map

A point is an asset of the record, coloured by its stage. A line is a physical link between two assets; its colour is the product. A dashed line is a link after the export stage. A link with no volume has one width: a volume is an aggregate of the research, not a fact of the record. A grey dotted line marked "in review" is a proposal, not a fact. A link from GEM is a GEM claim. Full text: Method page.
